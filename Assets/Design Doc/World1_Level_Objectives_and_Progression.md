# World 1 — GDD v3

The active first-ten recipes, star/pressure rules and five-phase boss are documented in [GDD v3 levels 1–20](../../Documentation/GDD-v3-Level01-20.md).

Levels: 1 First Spark (20 direct taps); 2 Quick Fingers (8 Small); 3 Side Step (5-hit streak); 4 Pulse Check (12 Small); 5 New Twist (10 moving); 6 Combo Craft (3 Golden); 7 Danger Read (15 rush hits); 8 Beat Shift (5 Priority and at most 2 Priority misses); 9 Skill Trial (15-hit streak); 10 World Boss (five mandatory phases).

Normal durations are 45 seconds, boss 60. Pressure limit is four. Time rewards are optional and capped at +6 seconds. A score cannot bypass a gate. Coins and XP retain their authored amounts; every 100 XP increases player level. Practice grants no rewards or stars. The v1 objectives in repository history are not active requirements.
