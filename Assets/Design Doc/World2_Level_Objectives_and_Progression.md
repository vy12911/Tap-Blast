# World 2 Objectives and Progression (Levels 11–20: Combo City)

The supplied GDD v1.0 pages 12–13 define World 2 ("Combo City") as focusing on Chain Blast, high-density target clusters, combo mastery, hazard introduction, and the World 2 Finale Boss ("Titan Core").

## Mechanics Breakdown

- **Level 11 ("CHAIN SPARK")**: Introduces fast spawn pacing (0.55s interval) and cluster density to build rapid combo multipliers.
- **Level 12 ("DOUBLE IMPACT")**: Combines target motion with a 20-hit streak objective.
- **Level 13 ("MEGA BURST")**: High-frequency Golden target spawns (30% probability) yielding 100 base points each.
- **Level 14 ("DRIFT CLUSTER")**: Moving targets cycling between 135px, 150px, and 165px sizes.
- **Level 15 ("COMBO FEVER")**: Accelerated spawn pace starting at 0.60s down to 0.40s over 20 seconds.
- **Level 16 ("TIME SURGE")**: Time Bubble collection goal (4 bubbles required, max 8 budget, +3s bonus per bubble).
- **Level 17 ("PRECISION MASTER")**: Small target sizes (120px–135px) awarding 15 base points per tap.
- **Level 18 ("GOLDEN STRIKE")**: Golden target collection goal (5 Golden targets required, 120 base points each).
- **Level 19 ("ULTRA STREAK")**: Accelerated moving spawns (0.50s down to 0.35s) demanding a 30-hit streak.
- **Level 20 ("TITAN CORE")**: World 2 Finale Boss featuring a 300px, 10-hit Titan Core with a visible health ring, accompanied by moving target waves.

## Completion and Mastery

- Stars and rewards follow standard rules:
  - 1 Star: Achieve the primary objective.
  - 2 Stars: Achieve the primary objective + meet the 2-star score threshold.
  - 3 Stars: Achieve 2-star score threshold + zero target misses + meet the 3-star combo threshold.
- Replaying any unlocked World 2 level awards the full coin and XP payout upon success.
