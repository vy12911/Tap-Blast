Brand

Tap Blast logo

Two wordmark directions built from the game's own pieces: the glossy blue target with its gold star, golden pop sparkles, and the indigo-to-violet brand gradient. Each is shown on the lavender menus, the indigo arena and a white card, then inside the game.

A · Candy Pop

Stacked and bubbly. Gold TAP over brand-gradient BLAST, glossy candy letters with a white rim and a deep indigo outline. A target bursts off the P in a shower of the same golden sparkles the game uses when you tap.

B · Arcade Burst

One line with forward motion. White letters with a heavy indigo outline on a gold explosion, with a target streaking in from the left. Reads well wide and short, so it suits banners and the store feature graphic.

App icon

The target on its own: no lettering, so it stays crisp at home-screen sizes. A tap ripple and sparkles say what the game is about at a glance.

App icon with the name

The same target and sunburst, now carrying TAP BLAST three ways. The lettering reads from about 76 px up, which covers the store listing and most launchers; at the smallest sizes the target still carries the icon.

In the game

Candy Pop on the level select and briefing; the compact one-line version fits the top bar where space is tight.

Why these choices

It's the game's own targetThe blue ball with the gold star is the thing players tap thousands of times, so the logo and the gameplay recognise each other.
Brand colours, not new onesGold for rewards and the indigo-violet gradient from the buttons and results stage. Nothing new to keep in sync.
Reads on every surfaceThe thick indigo outline and white rim keep the letters legible on the lavender menus, the dark arena and plain white.
Clean edgesDrawn as vectors, so exports have crisp transparent edges with no glow fringe, at any size.
