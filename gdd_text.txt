 TAP BLAST
 FULL GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT (GDD)
Mobile Casual Arcade  One-Touch Reaction  Tap / Combo / Chain / Risk & Reward
Version 1.0  Production Design
Document Purpose
This document defines the complete gameplay, progression, UX, economy, monetization, content, audio/visual direction, analytics,
technical architecture and production requirements for Tap Blast. It is intended to be usable as a working reference for a Unity
development team.
1. High Concept
Tap Blast is a fast one-touch arcade game in which players tap glowing targets, build combo multipliers, avoid hazards, execute
timing-perfect taps, create chain reactions, activate power-ups and chase increasingly difficult level objectives. A round is short
enough for casual play but deep enough to reward mastery.
2. Design Pillars
 Instant understanding: the first interaction should teach the game without a long tutorial.
 
 Juicy feedback: every tap communicates success through motion, sound, score popups and particles.
 
 Risk and reward: valuable targets should sometimes require the player to take a calculated risk.
 
 Mastery: players can improve accuracy, timing, combo management and route selection.
 
 Progression: new mechanics appear throughout the campaign instead of only increasing speed.
 
 Short-session replayability: a failed round should naturally produce the desire for another attempt.
 
 Readable chaos: difficulty may become intense, but target identity must remain clear.
 
3. Core Player Experience
The intended emotional sequence:
See target -> Tap -> Pop -> Combo rises -> More targets appear -> Risk appears -> Player makes a decision -> Chain/Power
activates -> Score explodes -> Reward -> 'One more try'
The player should feel increasingly powerful as the combo grows, while the presence of Bombs, timing targets and disappearing
high-value targets prevents mindless tapping.
4. Game Modes
MVP recommendation: launch with Campaign + Daily Challenge. Add Endless and event modes after core retention is proven.
5. Core Round Flow
 Load level configuration.
 
 Show objective and reward preview for a brief moment.
 
 Start countdown / immediate play.
 
 Spawn targets according to the level pattern.
 
 Track taps, misses, hazards, combo, score, power-ups and objectives.
 
 Trigger scripted or probability-based events.
 
 Allow Ultimate Blast when meter reaches 100%.
 
 Run Final Blast phase where configured.
 
 Stop spawning when time expires or the level's completion condition is met.
 
 Calculate stars, rewards, mission progress and high-score status.

6. Target System
7. Target Spawn Rules
 Targets must not spawn directly under critical HUD elements.
 
 Maintain minimum spacing between simultaneous targets unless a Chain opportunity is intentionally created.
 
 Avoid impossible overlaps between Bombs and required targets.
 
 Spawn probability is controlled per level so designers can author difficulty.
 
 Target lifetime prevents the arena from becoming permanently saturated.
 
 A difficulty curve can modify spawn rate, lifetime, size, movement speed and hazard probability independently.
 
8. Scoring System
Suggested base formula:
Final Tap Score = Base Target Value x Combo Multiplier x Temporary Score Multiplier + Bonuses
Designers should be able to tune these values per mode without changing gameplay code.
9. Combo System
 Every valid target tap increments the combo.
 
 A missed required target can reset or reduce combo depending on the level rule.
 
 Bomb taps immediately break combo in standard mode.
 
 Optional Shield can protect the combo once.
 
 Combo thresholds trigger increasingly strong audiovisual feedback.
 
 A combo should be visually obvious in the HUD at all times.
 
10. Chain Blast
Chain Blast is a signature system. Designers deliberately create clusters in which fast, accurate tapping can cause cascading
explosions. The player is rewarded for recognizing spatial relationships.
11. Perfect Tap
 Target transitions: BIG -> MEDIUM -> SMALL -> PERFECT.
 
 The perfect window is visually highlighted.
 
 Perfect timing gives maximum score and Ultimate charge.
 
 Early/late taps may give reduced rewards in beginner levels.
 
 Advanced levels can require multiple Perfect Taps consecutively.
 
 Moving Perfect targets combine timing and reaction skill.
 
12. Bomb & Hazard System
 Bombs are visually distinct enough to be fair but should create tension.
 
 Bomb probability rises slowly through the campaign.
 
 Bombs can move, hide among clusters or appear during Frenzy in advanced levels.
 
 A bomb hit normally resets combo and may subtract a small score amount.
 
 Never create a required path that forces an unavoidable bomb tap.

14. Ultimate Meter
The Ultimate Meter fills through successful taps, Perfect Taps, Chain Blasts and selected bonuses. At 100%, the player may activate
Ultimate Blast. The button should become visually prominent without blocking targets.
 Valid tap: small charge
 
 Perfect: larger charge
 
 Chain: major charge
 
 Golden/Diamond: bonus charge
 
 Bomb: no charge
 
 Ultimate clears eligible normal/special targets according to level rules
 
15. Special Events
16. Level Design Rules
 Each level should have a clear primary objective.
 
 Most levels should have one secondary mastery condition.
 
 Introduce one new mechanic at a time.
 
 After introducing a mechanic, provide at least one level where it is the main focus before combining it with others.
 
 Boss levels should test a mechanic combination rather than simply multiplying speed.
 
 Difficulty spikes should be preceded by a learning level.
 
 The final 5–10 seconds of many levels should feel more intense than the opening.
 
17. 100-Level Campaign
18. Detailed Early Levels (1–20)
19. Levels 21–100: Progression Summary
Levels 21–100 should progressively combine the systems described above. Difficulty should be tuned using spawn rate, target
lifetime, target size, movement speed, hazard chance, Perfect window, event frequency and objective requirements rather than only
increasing speed.
20. Boss / Challenge Design
 Mini Boss: one giant target with a visible health/progress ring.
 
 Chain Master: requires several successful chain reactions.
 
 Danger Boss: bomb patterns must be navigated safely.
 
 Power Boss: power-up timing is central.
 
 Perfect Boss: multiple timing windows.
 
 Frenzy Boss: high-speed target waves.
 
 Chaos Boss: multiple mechanics simultaneously.
 
 Master Trial: strict accuracy and combo requirements.
 
 Ultimate Challenge: multi-phase 60-second finale.
 
21. Level 100 Ultimate Challenge
Result screen should display Score, Best Combo, Perfect Taps, Chain Count, Coins, Gems, Stars and a 'Personal Best' indicator.

25. Economy
Keep score progression independent from paid power so competitive or leaderboard results remain credible.
26. Cosmetic Collection
 Bubble skins: Neon, Fire, Ice, Galaxy, Plasma, Toxic, Rainbow, Diamond, Shadow.
 
 Blast effects: Basic, Lightning, Shockwave, Black Hole, Rainbow, Galaxy.
 
 Trails: Spark, Fire, Rainbow, Electric.
 
 Arena themes: Neon City, Deep Space, Ocean, Cyber, Candy, Galaxy.
 
 UI themes can be added later.
 
 Cosmetics should provide visual personalization, not unfair scoring advantages.
 
27. Treasure Box System
Treasure Boxes provide a collection/reward layer. Boxes can contain Coins, Gems, cosmetic fragments, complete cosmetics or
temporary boosters.
 Common Box: frequent, mostly Coins.
 
 Rare Box: Coins + Gems + cosmetic chance.
 
 Epic Box: stronger cosmetic odds.
 
 Event Box: limited-time themed cosmetics.
 
 Avoid confusing or deceptive reward presentation; show clear reward categories and probabilities where required.

29. Daily Challenge
Every day, one curated level uses a fixed seed and rule set so players can compare their performance with their own previous
attempt and, if implemented later, a leaderboard.
 Fixed target sequence/seed
 
 One special modifier
 
 Daily reward for completion
 
 Personal-best tracking
 
 Optional leaderboard after backend infrastructure is available
 
30. Endless / Survival Mode
 No fixed level goal.
 
 Difficulty continuously increases.
 
 Three mistakes can end the run.
 
 Best score becomes the main progression metric.
 
 Milestones can award Coins/Gems.
 
 Ideal for later retention content.
 
31. UI / Screen List
32. Gameplay HUD
Top: Score | Timer. Center: gameplay arena. Bottom: Combo and Ultimate Meter. Power-up buttons should appear only when
relevant. Pause should remain accessible but not distract from targets.
33. UX Principles
 Tap targets must be large enough for comfortable mobile interaction.
 
 Critical targets must have distinct shapes as well as colors so color alone is not the only identifier.
 
 Avoid placing targets under system gesture zones.
 
 Use short animations that never hide the next target for too long.
 
 Provide haptic feedback for major events where supported.
 
 Use clear result messaging: NEW BEST, PERFECT, COMBO, GREAT, MISS, BOMB, etc.
 
34. Accessibility
 Do not rely on color alone to distinguish Bombs and valid targets.
 
 Offer reduced-motion mode.
 
 Offer vibration toggle.
 
 Use readable HUD typography.
 
 Maintain adequate contrast between targets and arena.
 
 Support device aspect ratios safely using responsive UI anchors.
 
35. Art Direction
Visual identity: dark or rich background with luminous targets, soft bloom/glow, clean HUD panels and energetic particle effects. The
target should remain the visual focal point.
 Use a small controlled color family for target categories.

 Use particle bursts, rings, shockwaves and score popups to make successful play feel physical.
 
 Avoid permanent excessive glow that reduces readability.

37. Audio Design
 Every target type gets a recognizable sound family.
 
 Combo thresholds increase musical intensity.
 
 Perfect Tap gets a distinct high-pitch confirmation.
 
 Bomb gets a warning/error sound.
 
 Chain Blast uses layered impacts.
 
 Ultimate Blast gets the strongest audio event.
 
 Separate Music, SFX and Haptics volume controls.
 
38. Monetization Strategy
Do not interrupt active gameplay with ads. Rewarded ads should be clearly optional and should not punish players who decline.

missed day. Avoid overly punishing long-term players.

48. Game States
Recommended state machine:
Boot -> Home -> PreLevel -> Countdown -> Playing -> Paused -> Resuming -> Results -> NextLevel / Replay -> Home
Failure can transition to Results or an optional Continue flow before Results.
49. Tutorial
 Level 1: show a single glowing target and animated tap hint.
 
 After first tap: explain score.
 
 After several taps: introduce Combo.
 
 Level 6: introduce Golden target.
 
 Level 8: introduce Time Bubble.
 
 Level 11: introduce Chain Blast.
 
 Level 16: introduce Bomb with a safe example.
 
 Later systems should be introduced contextually rather than through long tutorial screens.
 
50. Difficulty Formula
Difficulty should be a weighted combination rather than a single speed value.
Difficulty ≈ Spawn Pressure + Movement + Target Size Reduction + Hazard Density + Timing Precision + Objective Pressure
 Early worlds emphasize learning.
 
 Middle worlds emphasize combinations.
 
 Late worlds emphasize decision-making and mastery.
 
 When adding a difficult mechanic, temporarily reduce another difficulty dimension to keep the level fair.

 Show the reason: TIME UP, BOMB HIT, OBJECTIVE MISSED, etc.

 Never require watching an ad to continue.

56. QA Checklist
 Targets never spawn off-screen.
 
 Required target never becomes impossible to tap.
 
 Bomb is always visually distinguishable.
 
 Combo resets correctly.
 
 Ultimate meter cannot exceed 100%.
 
 Power-up timers stop/reset correctly.
 
 Pause freezes gameplay state correctly.
 
 Restart clears all active targets, timers and effects.
 
 Level completion cannot trigger twice.
 
 Rewards cannot be granted twice for one completion.
 
 Save data survives app restart.
 
 Ads do not appear during active gameplay.
 
 All screen aspect ratios remain usable.

59. Recommended MVP Scope
For the first playable version, do NOT build all 100 levels and all meta systems immediately. Build a polished 10-level vertical slice
first.
 Normal + Golden + Bomb + Time targets
 
 Combo system
 
 Basic Chain Blast
 
 One power-up: Freeze
 
 Score and timer
 
 Three-star result screen
 
 10 handcrafted levels
 
 Basic sound/VFX/haptics
 
 Restart/replay flow
 
If players enjoy replaying the 10-level slice, expand to Perfect, Frenzy, Ultimate, missions, collection and the remaining campaign.
60. Final Product Vision
Tap Blast should be extremely easy to start but progressively difficult to master. The player should always have a visible next goal:
beat the score, protect the combo, create a bigger chain, hit a Perfect window, earn three stars, complete a mission, unlock a
cosmetic, or beat a personal best. The strongest version of the game is not simply faster tapping—it is a readable arcade system
where every tap represents a small decision and every successful sequence builds toward a spectacular payoff.
